SMARTER ENERGY HABITS

Energy Tips.

Simple habits can help reduce unnecessary electricity consumption without changing the way you live.

💡

Use efficient lighting

Prefer LED bulbs, use natural daylight when possible and switch lights off in empty rooms.

❄️

Cool efficiently

Keep AC settings reasonable, clean filters regularly and avoid cooling empty rooms.

🔌

Cut unnecessary use

Switch appliances off when they are not needed instead of leaving them running unnecessarily.

🧺

Run smarter loads

Avoid very small washing-machine loads and use energy-saving modes when available.
